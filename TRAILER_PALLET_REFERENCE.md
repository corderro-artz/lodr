# US Ground Trailer Pallet Layout Reference

Below are **standard US dry van trailers** and, for each, the **typical loading pattern** for the three common US pallet sizes:

- **48 × 40 in** (GMA)  
- **42 × 42 in** (square)  
- **48 × 48 in** (square)

Assumptions (typical interiors):

- **53 ft dry van:** length ≈ **636 in**, width ≈ **100 in**  
- **48 ft dry van:** length ≈ **576 in**, width ≈ **100 in**  
- **28 ft pup trailer:** length ≈ **318 in**, width ≈ **100 in**  

All layouts assume pallets are placed **flat on the floor**, no stacking.

---

## 1. 53 ft Dry Van (≈636 in × 100 in)

### 1.1 Pallet: 48 × 40 in (GMA)

**Orientation (standard):**

- **48 in side along trailer length**  
- **40 in side across trailer width**

**Columns across width:**

- Width used by 2 pallets:  
  \( 40 + 40 = 80 \text{ in} \)  
- Remaining lateral clearance:  
  \( 100 - 80 = 20 \text{ in} \Rightarrow 10 \text{ in each side} \)  

→ **2 columns**

**Rows along length:**

- Rows:  
  \( 636 \div 48 \approx 13.25 \Rightarrow 13 \text{ full rows} \)  
- Length used:  
  \( 13 \times 48 = 624 \text{ in} \)  
- Remaining longitudinal clearance:  
  \( 636 - 624 = 12 \text{ in} \)  

**Result:**

- **Layout:** 2 columns × 13 rows  
- **Total pallets:** **26**  
- **Clearance:** ~12 in front/back, ~10 in each side
